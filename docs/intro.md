# Introduction

Welcome to the Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics textbook. This comprehensive resource covers the fundamental concepts and advanced topics in physical AI and humanoid robotics.

## About This Textbook

This textbook is organized into several modules, each covering a specific aspect of physical AI and humanoid robotics:

- **Foundations**: Basic concepts in mathematics, physics, and control theory
- **Robotics Math**: Kinematics, dynamics, and transformations
- **Control Systems**: Classical and modern control approaches
- **ROS 2**: Robot Operating System concepts and development
- **Sensor Fusion**: Techniques for combining multiple sensor inputs
- **Simulation**: Tools and techniques for robot simulation
- **AI Agents**: Machine learning and AI for robotics
- **Humanoid Robotics**: Specialized topics in humanoid robot design and control

Each module is further divided into weekly content to guide your learning journey.
