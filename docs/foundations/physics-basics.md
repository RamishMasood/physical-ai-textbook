# Physics Basics

This section covers the fundamental physics concepts needed for robotics and AI.

## Mechanics

Key physics concepts for robotics:

- Newton's laws of motion
- Forces and torques
- Kinematics vs. dynamics
- Conservation of energy and momentum

## Electromagnetism

Electromagnetic principles relevant to robotics:

- Electric circuits
- Magnetic fields
- Electromagnetic induction
- Sensors based on electromagnetic principles
