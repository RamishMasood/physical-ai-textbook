# Humanoid Manipulation

This section covers manipulation techniques for humanoid robots.

## Kinematic Chains

Humanoid arms form kinematic chains with multiple degrees of freedom:

- Shoulder: 3 DOF (yaw, pitch, roll)
- Elbow: 1 DOF (flexion/extension)
- Wrist: 3 DOF (yaw, pitch, roll)

## Grasp Planning

Types of grasps:

- **Power grasp**: For holding heavy objects
- **Precision grasp**: For fine manipulation
- **Pinch grasp**: For small objects

## Control Strategies

- **Impedance control**: Control the robot's mechanical impedance
- **Admittance control**: Control the robot's response to external forces
- **Hybrid force/position control**: Combine force and position control

## Challenges

- Redundancy resolution
- Collision avoidance
- Force control
- Object recognition and grasping
