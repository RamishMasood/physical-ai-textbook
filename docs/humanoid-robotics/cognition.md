# Humanoid Cognition

This section covers cognitive capabilities for humanoid robots.

## Perception Systems

Humanoid robots need to perceive and understand their environment:

- **Vision**: Object recognition, scene understanding
- **Audition**: Speech recognition, sound localization
- **Tactile sensing**: Touch and force feedback
- **Proprioception**: Body position awareness

## Memory and Learning

Cognitive architectures for humanoid robots:

- **Episodic memory**: Remembering specific events
- **Semantic memory**: General knowledge
- **Working memory**: Active information processing

## Decision Making

Cognitive models for decision making:

- **Reactive systems**: Direct response to stimuli
- **Deliberative systems**: Planning and reasoning
- **Hybrid architectures**: Combining reactive and deliberative approaches

## Human-Robot Interaction

Key aspects of humanoid cognition:

- **Theory of mind**: Understanding human intentions
- **Social cognition**: Understanding social norms
- **Emotional intelligence**: Recognizing and responding to emotions
