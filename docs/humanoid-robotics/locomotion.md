# Humanoid Locomotion

This section covers the principles of humanoid robot locomotion.

## Walking Patterns

Humanoid walking involves complex control to maintain balance:

- Double support phase: Both feet on ground
- Single support phase: One foot on ground
- Swing phase: Foot in motion

## Balance Control

Key techniques for maintaining balance:

- Zero Moment Point (ZMP)
- Capture Point
- Linear Inverted Pendulum Model (LIPM)

## Gait Generation

Methods for generating stable gaits:

- Pre-programmed trajectories
- Model-based control
- Learning-based approaches

## Challenges

- Dynamic balance
- Disturbance rejection
- Terrain adaptation
- Energy efficiency
