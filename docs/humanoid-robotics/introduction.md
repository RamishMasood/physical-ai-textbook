# Introduction to Humanoid Robotics

This section provides an introduction to humanoid robotics - robots with human-like characteristics.

## What are Humanoid Robots?

Humanoid robots are robots that have a human-like body structure with a head, torso, arms, and legs.

## Key Challenges

- **Balance**: Maintaining stability on two legs
- **Locomotion**: Walking, running, and other forms of movement
- **Manipulation**: Using hands for complex tasks
- **Cognition**: Understanding and interacting with the environment

## Applications

- Service robotics
- Research platforms
- Entertainment
- Healthcare assistance
